System Architecture & Overview

FlowLedger AI operates as an autonomous, multi-rail receivables agent. Instead of requiring human bookkeeping oversight, it listens to communication triggers, creates structured ledger payloads, verifies compliance, and enforces receivables collection.

High-Level Ingestion Pipeline
User Trigger (Chat / Calendar / Slack) → LLM Token Extractor → Canonical Invoice JSON → Dispatcher (Email / WhatsApp) → Webhook Settlement

2. Natural Language Parsing Specification

FlowLedger uses high-throughput lightweight LLMs (GPT-4o mini / Gemini Flash) to parse conversational text into valid, RFC-compliant accounting payloads.

3. Real Email & Webhook Dispatch Pipeline

The email system bypasses local email client popups (`mailto:`) by transmitting authenticated HTTP payload requests through cloud messaging relays directly into recipient inboxes.

Subject Rule
pay me the bill

Strictly configured subject line matching requirements.

Dynamic Body Matter
- Bill Amount Due: $X,XXX
- Deadline to Pay Off: [Date]

Includes polite greeting and clear settlement deadlines.

4. Smart Relationship Chaser Protocol

Unlike standard automation engines that blindly send repeated notices, the FlowLedger AI engine adapts its schedule based on client AP payment history and immediately halts reminders when an inline question is lodged.

  • Dispute Lockout: Client question pauses automated notifications for 48 hours.
  • AP Habit Tracking: Respects Friday and month-end corporate payroll payment routines.
  • Tone Modulation: Transitions gracefully from helpful reminder to formal inquiry.
