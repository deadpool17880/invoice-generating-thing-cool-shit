# FlowLedger AI ⚡
> **Autonomous "Invoice-to-Cash" Receivables Intelligence Platform for Micro-SMEs**

FlowLedger AI solves the broken, fragmented invoice lifecycle for micro and small B2B service businesses (agencies, consultants, developers, contractors). Instead of forcing business owners to use bloated accounting dashboards, FlowLedger acts as an **autonomous billing employee**.

---

## 🚀 Key Standout Features

1. **Conversational Invoicing (Zero Forms)**
   - Type or message naturally: *"Bill Acme Corp ₹75,000 for brand identity sprint due in 14 days"*
   - AI extracts items, tax compliance, due dates, and dispatches the link.

2. **1-Click Frictionless Client Payment Portal**
   - Multi-rail checkout: Instant ACH / Direct Debit, UPI, Apple Pay, Cards, Wise international.
   - Inline dispute & question triage directly on the invoice link (auto-pauses reminder alarms while questions are resolved).

3. **Smart Relationship-Aware Chaser**
   - AI learns client payout habits (e.g. paying every alternate Friday) and tailors reminder cadences to avoid relationship friction.

4. **Zero-Touch Bank Feed Auto-Reconciliation**
   - Webhook & open-banking transaction matching to clear invoices instantly when funds arrive.

5. **Disruptive Unit Economics**
   - AI token cost is under **₹0.10 (10 paise) per invoice** using GPT-4o mini / Gemini Flash.
   - 95%+ gross profit margins with a simple micro-brokerage model (0.5% or ₹20/invoice).
